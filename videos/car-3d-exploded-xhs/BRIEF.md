# Creative brief — Code Exploded / XHS cut

- **Platform:** Xiaohongshu video note
- **Canvas:** 1080 × 1440 (3:4), 30 fps
- **Duration:** 5.6 seconds, seamless loop
- **Hook:** A complete concept car separates into rigid mechanical groups and magnetically reassembles. No particle morph and no generated transition video.
- **Look:** Graphite body, restrained titanium structure, orange brake accents, moving cyan/orange reflections, nearly black automotive studio.
- **Typography:** None. The car is the only subject.
- **Framing:** Assembled car fills roughly 88% of the width. The camera pulls back only while parts separate, then pushes in during the observation hold.
- **Motion:** 220° whip orbit, hard brake, 0.65-second three-beat rigid-body explosion, 0.8-second observation drift, magnetic collapse, reverse orbit to the opening frame.
- **Sound direction for final render:** low sub hit, short servo ticks per component tier, wide mechanical whoosh, magnetic snap on closure. No narration required.

Render/export is intentionally deferred until the user approves the preview.
